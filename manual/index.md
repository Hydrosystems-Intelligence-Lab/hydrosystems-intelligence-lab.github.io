## Purpose

This handbook is the practical companion to the public Abeshu Hydrosystems Intelligence Group website. The public website presents who we are and what we work on. This handbook collects the shared knowledge that helps the group work well together: expectations, workflows, technical recipes, writing guidance, reusable templates, and professional development resources.

The handbook should grow with the research group. When a process, tutorial, template, or recurring answer would help future group members, it belongs here.

## Structure

The structure follows the same useful logic as strong research group handbooks: start with values and identity, then move into practical onboarding, research workflows, computing, writing, and career development.

- [About the Group]({{ '/manual/about/' | relative_url }}) introduces the group mission, values, scientific communities, and first actions for new members.
- [How to Edit This Handbook]({{ '/manual/how-to-edit/' | relative_url }}) explains how to improve the handbook through normal website edits.
- [Onboarding]({{ '/manual/onboarding/' | relative_url }}) lists first-week setup steps and common access needs.
- [Graduate Studies Matters]({{ '/manual/graduate-studies/' | relative_url }}) collects guidance for milestones, advising, and academic progress.
- [Advising Philosophy and Expectations]({{ '/manual/advising-philosophy-and-expectations/' | relative_url }}) outlines mentoring norms, milestones, authorship expectations, and professional development support.
- [PhD Progress Rubric]({{ '/manual/phd-progress-rubric/' | relative_url }}) defines competency expectations across research, teaching, coursework, and professionalism from Year 1 through defense.
- [Research Workflows]({{ '/manual/research-workflows/' | relative_url }}) covers project organization, reproducibility, meetings, and archiving.
- [General Programming]({{ '/manual/programming/' | relative_url }}) covers computing environments, Git, notebooks, and code quality.
- [Writing and Reviewing]({{ '/manual/writing/' | relative_url }}) covers manuscripts, proposals, talks, peer review, and internal feedback.
- [Group Software]({{ '/manual/software/' | relative_url }}) will document group-developed tools and repositories.
- [Other Software]({{ '/manual/other-software/' | relative_url }}) collects external tools used across projects.
- [Data Visualization]({{ '/manual/data-visualization/' | relative_url }}) captures plotting standards and figure design habits.
- [Job Search]({{ '/manual/job-search/' | relative_url }}) supports fellowships, academic jobs, industry roles, and applications.
- [Templates]({{ '/manual/templates/' | relative_url }}) stores reusable project, meeting, review, and writing templates.
- [Posts and Resources]({{ '/manual/resources/' | relative_url }}) points to tutorials, blog posts, and reference material.

## Contributing

Treat the handbook as a shared group asset. Small edits are encouraged when they make a process clearer, remove outdated information, or add a reusable resource.

Good handbook entries are specific, short enough to maintain, and written for a future group member who is trying to get real work done.
